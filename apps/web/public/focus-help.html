ONE THING AT A TIME

Using Orin to focus

You don’t need a perfect plan. Start with what you have, and decide the next step when you’re ready.

Start with one thing

  1. Open Tasks and choose Add one thing.
  2. Write a task, worry, or idea in your own words. Optional details can wait.
  3. Choose Save and continue. Your task appears on Now.
  4. Choose Start when you’re ready. You can finish, make the step smaller, or switch tasks.

Plan only if it helps

Today lets you choose low, medium, or high energy and keep up to three tasks. Skip the energy check if you prefer. You can change your plan later.

Ask Orin in your own words

Open Ask Orin and choose an example, or type a request. Examples help you capture a task, set your energy, choose today’s tasks, start focus, log a distraction, or close the day. Replace [task name] in an example with the title of your task.

Keep your choices private

Drift notes and reflections stay in your account. In Tasks → Progress and task review → Your private focus data, you can export or delete focus settings, daily plans, drift notes, and reflections. This does not delete your tasks or account.

Releasing a task asks for approval. You can restore a released task from the review screen. Orin does not send reminders or messages to contacts; focus features support your work and are not medical treatment.
